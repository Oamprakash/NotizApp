Notiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Applies to the Notiz app on Android and iOS

Notiz helps a housing society share notices, collect maintenance dues, handle complaints, run polls and chat — instead of WhatsApp groups. This page explains what data Notiz handles, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

In short: we collect only what the society features need. Your data is visible to your own society (and some of it only to its committee). We don't sell data, show ads or track you across other apps. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

We do not collect your contacts, location, card or bank details, or UPI PIN. Notiz never sees your card or UPI details: online payments are handled by Razorpay on the society's own Razorpay account, and UPI payments happen in your UPI app.

Services we use

  • Supabase — database, login and photo storage (our servers).
  • SMS provider — delivers the login code to your number.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service — delivers notifications.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reports.
  • Razorpay — online dues payments, when your society turns them on. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to payment details you enter there.

Each processes data only to provide its service to Notiz.

Your society's role

Your society's committee (admins) decides who joins, posts notices, raises bills, confirms payments and can moderate chat (remove messages, mute members). Paid dues and receipts are the society's financial records.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. If you leave a society, you lose access to it, but what you posted stays for the society. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Delete your account

In the app: open Notiz → Settings (gear icon) → Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm. This happens immediately.

Without the app: email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] from any address, giving the mobile number you log in with. We'll confirm by SMS and delete the account within 7 days.

What's deleted: your name, mobile number, memberships, notification tokens, language setting and notice read history.

What stays (without your name): notices, complaints, poll votes and chat messages you posted remain visible to the society as "a member". Dues and receipts belong to the flat and stay as the society's financial records.

If you are the only admin of a society that has other members, make another member admin first.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us for a summary of your data, correct it (you can edit your name in Settings) or delete it, and raise a grievance with our grievance officer at the contact above. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database access rules make sure each person sees only their own society's data, and complaint photos only reach the person who raised them and the admins.

Children

Notiz is meant for adult residents and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
